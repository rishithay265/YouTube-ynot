## L1-350
- Opening paradox: Amma is Tripura-sundari, Shiva is Tripur-antaka; the Purana keeps the three asuras nameless in the title ("Tripurasura samhara"), Shiva is called Tripurantaka / Tripurari, not "killer of X, Y, Z". Asks why: the tattva behind the leela.
- Tarakasura (killed by Subrahmanya) had 3 sons: eldest TARAKAKSHA, second KAMALAKSHA, third VIDYUNMALI. (NB: SP ch.1 (Shastri) has Vidyunmali middle, Kamalaksha youngest.)
- Tarakasura's trick: asked to die only by a son born of Shiva, believing Shiva (Kamari, enemy of Kama) would never father one; Subrahmanya was born anyway.
- Lesson he draws: the sons should have learnt from their father's mistake (he climbed the last step, tapas, Brahma appeared, then asked the wrong thing). Contrast: Prahlada learnt (heard Narada in Leelavati's womb), and Prahlada's poem pleading for Bali (Potana Bhagavatam: 'ఇతనికి నింద్ర పదంబిచ్చి...' = what use is Indra's post; your feet are the real wealth).
- Purana's purpose: show one mistake and its consequences again and again: devotee = protection, enemy of Bhagavan = punishment.
- The three sons did ugra tapas to Brahma; asked: "we must not die". Brahma: the atman never dies, the body must; a born body has a departure date; (long sermon: jagat = jayate + gacchati; EAMCET 3-hour exam analogy: human birth is the 3-hour exam).
- They mock Brahma: call him "సరసిజ సూతి" (born of a lotus) = you were born, so you will go too.

## L351-700
- Rakshasa trait: speech that wounds worse than sawing a belly without anaesthesia; their reply to Brahma is a Telugu padyam full of sarcasm: "Sarasija-suti" (lotus-born, i.e. you too were born).
- Their argument (padyam, 'తీర భోగముల్ ...'): you give wealth, kingdoms, pleasures; what use if the body that enjoys them doesn't last? Someone else will eat what we leave; that is their pain ("I must eat it, no one else").
- Aside: Appayya Dikshitar, "I took 3 hours per shloka, so I thought of You longer; I am blessed."
- Brahma: "That is the nature of the world, decided by Parabrahman. Fine, you choose HOW you will die, I'll grant it." (Like Bhishma's svacchanda-marana.) He praises Brahma's big-heartedness; says he always says "Brahma-garu" with respect: Brahma teaches gyana to every asura who appears before him.
- Aside: Brahma/Vishnu/Shiva are one Brahman given the "big seat" in turn, like the groom at a wedding / grandfather at shashtipurti; don't beat your chest over sectarian slights.
- Key teaching: accepting "death is certain" stops a man from sin; thinking "this will stay" is where rakshasatvam begins and the root of sin.
- The boon (padyam 'అయినను నీవు కాదనినా ... పైడి వెండి ఇనుప నగరంబులు మూడు ...'): "We tapas-ed to you, you came and said choose yourselves, our karma." Ask: call MAYA, build a GOLD city, a SILVER city, an IRON city; we three brothers ride one each; the three must keep moving in the sky without stopping; while they move we'll defeat everyone.
- Mockery of the vanity: enjoy pleasure sitting in one place? No, they must enjoy while circling so others see (imported powder tin shown off at the gate). "Tirugu kallu tirugutayi."
- The death condition: the three puras must never come in a straight line; once in 1000 divine years for ONE KSHANA they come into a straight line; then at high noon, ABHIJIT muhurta, moon in PUSHYAMI, rain falling from PUSHKALAVARTAKA clouds; one who rides a NEW chariot never ridden before, holds an UNPRECEDENTED bow never held before, a DEVATA-SARVABHAUMA (lord of gods), with ONE arrow released at once, strikes while they're in line: then we die. If not in that kshana, we live on again.
- Brahma: "Aha, how much you thought in so little time!" Tathastu. Warns: "rare boon, nobody asked like this; handle with care." (Analogy: father yields to son's demand for a Hero Honda; 3rd day son in ICU, 2 years of treatment = asura boons.)
- After the boon: they go to AMARAVATI (Indra's city, the bhoga-sthanas), defeat all the devas. Tadatmya with body-strength: joy of "I am strong" needs someone proved weak, so they keep beating everyone needlessly; no fear of death (a gyani has no fear of death by wisdom; the agyani got it by a boon, which only raises his sin).
- They make the powerful their servants: order the rain-god (Indra/Varuna) to rain in high summer on command; order the SUN to stay mild when they go out ("if heat comes I'll finish you"); order the MOON to shine by day, the sun to set: they rule nature.

## L701-1050
- Asura rule breaks nature's order: seasons lose meaning ("in shishira no tree may drop a leaf; I want spring everywhere"). Why gods suffer: their power to protect the world goes unused / is misused out of fear; they must do what they shouldn't and can't do what they should.
- Gods go to Brahma again (he gave the boon, he knows). Brahma's padyam ('వరము వారలకిడితి ... వరబలంబు ఆత్మీయ బలము శంభు పూజన ఫలంబు ఏకమై'): THREE strengths in them: (1) my boon, (2) their own bodily strength, (3) fruit of intense SHIVA-PUJA: they are Shiva-bhaktas, protected by Shiva. You and I together would lose. Answer must come from Shiva; gods only kill when dharma breaks; let's go to Shiva.
- Kailasa: "Shambho Shankara!" (Arta-rakshana). Shiva comes running, asks "what happened" (courtesy, he knows).
- Shiva's padyam ('...నిశాచర వీరులు భక్తులు ఎట్లు వారిని నశయింప చేతు నిక శ్రీపతి యొద్దకు పోయి చెప్పురా ... మాయగొని మీ పరితాపము బాపురు'): "True, they torment you, they must die. But from waking to night they chant my name, worship me, trust me; that's bhakti (misused, yes). To go and kill one while he is my parama-bhakta is against my nature. I will not kill." Also: they asked that the Devata-sarvabhauma (= Parameshvara) kill them, so only Shiva can. "I can't tell you, in this form, how my devotees lose their devotion to me; I also exist in another form; go to Sripati (Vishnu); he will do some MAYA and relieve you." Chaganti: not sarcasm; same Brahman; the asura thinks Shiva and Vishnu are different ("I'm Shankara-bhakta, not Vishnu-bhakta"), so it can come from that side. (Tirupati-contact analogy: "he'll do something, you'll get darshan".)
- Gods to VAIKUNTHA, praise Narayana; he appears; "Aha, Shankara sent you? Of course. Sit." He makes a sankalpa (maya = what he does that you can't know).
- From his sankalpa a PURUSHA is born (padyam 'ముండితమైన ... శిరమును కడుమాసిన ... వల్వలు ... ధర్మ ధర్మయని స్మరియించుచు'): shaven head, very dirty clothes, a soiled cloth over the shoulder, a SKULL (pothi? "పుర్రె") in hand(*), repeating "dharma, dharma" from birth.  (*SP: he holds a wooden water-pot and a broom/whisk, "a cloth in hand"; check.)
- Vishnu names him ARIHAN ('అరిహ నామము దాల్చి'). "Your teaching will be fully useful to Kali-purusha in Kali-yuga; for now one task."
- The moha-shastra: 16,000 shlokas (Chaganti: '16వేలుగా', '16వేల శ్లోకాలతో'; SP Shastri: 16 lakh? check) that sets Vedic dharma aside, beautiful to hear, "so easy, quick moksha", ruins whoever follows it: "poison smeared with honey". Go to the Tripura cities, sit like a great man and preach; slowly sanatanis drift; their dharma vanishes; then Shiva comes to kill.
- Twist (Chaganti): ARIHAN went, nobody listened; at the first shloka people said "shut up, Sanatana dharma is great" and walked off. He came back: "what shall I do?"
- Narayana: if an elder gives a CERTIFICATE, others believe. So for loka-rakshana: "NARADA, you must do this." Doctrine: a small adharma that reveals a big dharma doesn't count; tells Narada and Arihan "no fault comes to you; Tripura-samhara must happen."
- Narada goes first to the ELDEST, TARAKAKSHA (others will follow). Tarakaksha rises, bows, seats him: "Maharshi, why here?" Narada: "Don't you know? A mahapurusha has come to your kingdom, what a siddhanta, what diksha! I just took that diksha myself. Karana-janma!" "Where is he?" "Sitting at such a place." Summoned to the antahpura; asked to preach; the Tripuras listen because Narada's certificate is stamped.
- Chaganti: remember, what is the cause of the fall in Tripura-samhara? (Wrong teaching/ignorance.) Aside on bad books (a recent Ramayana book by a woman; dangerous ignorance in print; read with elders' permission; "untruth is very beautiful").

## L1051-1400  (Arihan's doctrine, as Chaganti voices it; he stresses "this is Arihan speaking, not me")
- "Samsara is truth: you, your wife, your children are real. 'Ishvara-kripa gave me children', what nonsense; you two made them. Who is Ishvara? Stop pujas to the unseen, yajnas, pouring ghee and milk."
- "Even four-faced Brahma was born like you and will go one day; he has a little power; he himself said 'I can't keep you'. You never even saw Shiva; why worship one you never saw?"
- "Punya is only this: feed the hungry (you see his relief), medicine for the sick, protect the frightened (abhaya). Yajna 'Om namo Narayanaya svaha', did Narayana take it? Did the one who said so see it?"
- "Let a student study whatever he wants. Wealth: 'cheating' is just what the less clever call the clever; bring in all you can and enjoy it; satisfy the senses, not agnihotra or devas."
- "Five jnanendriyas: see whatever you want, hear, speak, eat, smell, touch what you want; karmendriyas the same; mind and buddhi rejoice: THAT is Brahmananda. The one who renounced and sat alone only says there's bliss there so you won't pity him."
- padyam ('ఎప్పుడో పడిపోయెడు శరీరము ...'): the body will fall some day, so enjoy pleasures to the fullest; why limits, morals, your own rules? "This body likes that body: enjoy it. You're hungry and strong, he's weak: snatch and eat. Sin after death? Did you go and see? Bring one who came back."
- A Veda-accepting man would walk away ("faulty speech, he only trusts his eyes"). But the Tripuras listened with ears pricked: "What fools we were: pujas, Shivalingas, yajnas! We ourselves told Brahma 'no pleasure without life'."
- The king (Tarakaksha) proclaims to the whole kingdom: NO KINSHIP RULES ('వావి లేదు వరస లేదు'), no wife, no child: enjoy as you please. Bodily pleasure without bounds; no sin, no punya.
- padyam ('స్త్రీ పురుషులిట్టున పాలింపక పూర్వ ధర్మ పదవీ చ్యుతులై రేయి పవల్ ధన కామ ...'): men and women fell from their former dharma, day and night lusting for wealth and kama; sattva drained. No brahma-muhurta puja, no yajna, yaga, kratu, devas, havis, fear, shastra, rishi-vakya, purana. Abandoned all that is auspicious, embraced all inauspicious; the world filled with sin.
- Vishnu, Brahma and the gods go back to Shiva: "You sent us; everything is ready; there's no bhakti now; no one there worships you." Chaganti: NOT Vishnu's trickery; he shows where we get hurt: LISTENING TO WHAT ONE SHOULD NOT HEAR is the cause of ruin. Don't go to that hall; say "I won't listen, my Ishvara is with me." The fault is the listener's, not the speaker's. (Paramatma staged this for all yugas.) MORAL: don't hear bad words.
- Shiva: "Very good, the Tripuras have begun a 'great puja' (irony). Now I may kill them. Killing them is no great thing; they sinned. But as Devata-sarvabhauma: they asked 1000 divya years, Pushkaravartaka clouds, moon in Pushyami, midday Abhijit, a chariot, bow and ARROW never before; did you bring all these?" "We brought them, Prabhu." "Bring them."
- THE CHARIOT (essence of all worlds squeezed): EARTH made the chariot; RIGHT wheel SUN, LEFT wheel MOON; spokes: the 12 Adityas, six and six; decorated with STARS; shafts/poles to the horses: the SEASONS; SKY its canopy; MANDARA mountain Shiva's seat; UDAYA and ASTA mountains (rising/setting) the ... (మొదలుగా = axle-bases?); the YEARS its speed; UTTARAYANA and DAKSHINAYANA the linch-pins of the two wheels; the PANCHA-BHUTAS its strength; VEDAS, VEDANGAS, PURANAS its bells; GANGA and rivers as women with chamaras; BRAHMA charioteer; PRANAVA (Om) the whip; MERU (నీరు→మేరు) the BOW; ADISESHA the bowstring; SARASVATI the main bell; VISHNU the ARROW; the VEDAS the horses; MAHARSHIS walking ahead singing.
- Chaganti: "No more unjust/absurd chariot in the world" (and he grounds it in the SP: Shankara LAUGHED at the chariot: "what chariot is this?"). If earth is the chariot and it moves, everyone on it flies off; sun wheel comes, moon goes back (they can't roll together); Brahma never drove a chariot (his own driver analogy: "if my master drove I'd sit beside him... if I drove, it'd go in the sky"); Pranava as whip; Sarasvati clanging. Shiva: "fine, the children made it, let it be". Bhakti makes Ishvara yield, like our toy chariot of broad-beans (చిక్కుడుకాయ) and broom-sticks for Ratha Saptami that we ask the Sun to ride and then bin; the Sun still accepts.

- Chariot moves. The Tripuras laugh "vika vika" in their madness: "what can he hit in one kshana?"
- Shiva must hold Meru as bow, Adishesha as string, twang it, take Vishnu as arrow. Shiva's thought: "I am creator, sustainer, destroyer; whatever I do is grace. You think by Brahma's boon I can't kill you unless all this; you asked for a weapon not in the world: what if I open my THIRD EYE, what if I just look in anger? That isn't in Dhanurveda." His white face turned red; he resolved to burn them at once; then became compassion itself: "if I get angry at his ignorance, I'm like him"; laughed loudly, ocean of mercy.

## L1751-2100  (the tattva and the sadhana)
- Svapna: the mind creates (dream of being whipped, the master rescues you, wake up). Sushupti = laya: nothing known, all merge into the karana body. Whether you live 60, 80, 90 years, you only live these three avasthas through the three bodies.
- The FOURTH: TURIYA, the witness of the three. Ramana Maharshi asked "do you sleep?": "I am the witness of sleep." To reach Turiya, first PARAMA-BHAKTI.
- Without bhakti, maya makes the three look beautiful ("Sundari" = Tripura-sundari sense): wandering, eating out of boredom ('ఏమి తోచట్లేదు'), TV, and its "hand" the cellphone (Kali's essence heaped up), sleep and bed-coffee joys.
- "Fool, beyond these three there is a parama-karana: Bhagavan. Hold Ishvara's feet in bhakti, HE SHOOTS THE ARROW; when it hits, the three PURAS fall; then, with no mamakara for sthula/sukshma/karana, you watch all three as a witness. Lifting you there needs his DAYA, which comes by bhakti. So Tripura must be killed with DAYA, not anger: Tripurasura-samhara happened only by Shankara's boundless compassion."
- Adi Shankara, PRABODHA SUDHAKARA verse: "cetaś calatāṃ vihāya puraḥ sandhāya koṭi-dvayaṃ / tatraikatra nidhehi sarva-viṣayān anyatra ca śrīpatim / viśrānti-hitam asya kanuta(?)yor madhye tad ālokyatāṃ / yuktyā vā'nubhavena yatra paramānandasya tat sevyatām": the BALANCE (తక్కెడ / త్రాసు) analogy: put all worldly things in one pan and Sripati in the other; see which way the needle rests.
- Explanation: an empty balance quivers in any breeze; a 1 kg stone vs 100 g weights one by one, it never lifts. Put PARAMESHVARA in one pan; if no sorrow or joy lifts it ("in sorrow: my past sin, Ishvara took it; in joy: Ishvara's grace"), the needle stays: then Ishvara is ready to shoot the arrow for YOUR Tripura-samhara. At first it will sway (pretending otherwise is acting); bring it back, like the shopkeeper adjusting weights.
- Only SANATANA DHARMA did not ask "let death not come": it worships Mrityu as a devata (freeing you from a worn-out body into a new one). Don't ask that a ripe fruit not fall: MRITYUNJAYA MANTRA "Om tryambakam yajāmahe sugandhiṃ puṣṭi-vardhanam / urvārukam iva bandhanān mṛtyor mukṣīya māmṛtāt": released like a ripe cucumber from its stalk. (Contrast: the asuras' whole fall began with asking not to die.)
- SADHANA: every night before sleep, review: how many times did I waver, how many did I hold, how many did I answer smiling, how many did I grind my teeth? Then say your ishta-devata's name 11 times, Shiva-nama at sleep (he does the small laya), "Ishvara, tomorrow give me the strength not to snap/get angry," bow and sleep. Doing this again and again, the balance stays down: THEN Tripurasura-samhara happens (in you).
- Close: read with Shankara's Prabodha Sudhakara, this rakshasa-samhara shows our own ascent; one of Ishvara's greatest leelas.
