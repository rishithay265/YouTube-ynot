# Gita Press Shiva Mahapuran (Part 1, first half), Rudra Samhita, Yuddha Khanda ch. 1-12 ("त्रिपुरवधोपाख्यान")
Printed pp. 823-872 (PDF = printed + 10). Read from page images (no text layer). Source of every line below: that volume only.

## Where Chaganti garu's telling and the Gita Press text differ (label in script/description)
| Point | Gita Press SP | Chaganti garu |
|---|---|---|
| Brothers | Tarakaksha (eldest, gold, heaven), Vidyunmali (middle, iron, earth), Kamalaksha (youngest, silver, sky) (1.7-10, 57-58) | Kamalaksha second |
| Condition | midday Abhijit, moon in Pushya, Pushkara+Avarta clouds raining, after 1000 yrs; a god containing all gods, on an impossible chariot, one impossible arrow (1.48-52) | same, adds "1000 divine yrs", "never-before bow/chariot/arrow" |
| Shiva refuses | yes, twice: "वे दैत्य मेरे भक्त हैं" (3.1-7; 6.34-37) | yes |
| Bhuta-ganas from Vishnu's yajna burn like moths (ch.3) | in text | not told |
| Arihan | made by Vishnu from his own body; shaven, dirty clothes, wooden vessel, broom, mouth-cloth, "धर्म धर्म"; 16,000-verse Apabhramsha scripture; 4 disciples (ch.4) | shaven, dirty, "a skull" in hand, 16,000 verses |
| First failure | his maya fails because of their Shiva-worship (4.40) | nobody listens; walk away |
| Narada | takes initiation first, "certifies" him to the Tripura lord; lord + brothers initiated, then all citizens (4.42-63) | same, goes to Tarakaksha |
| Doctrine | no creator/karma; atma only Ishvara; अहिंसा परमो धर्मः; heaven/hell here; mocks animal sacrifice; enjoy body; caste false; Kashyapa married 13 sisters so marriage rules pointless (ch.5) | samsara is truth, see-only, enjoy senses, "this body likes that body", kinship-free proclamation |
| Sanskrit 5.35, 6.28 | "बौद्धागम..." (Hindi translation omits it / renders "वेदविरुद्ध") | never names any religion |
| Fall | wifely fidelity, shraddha, yajna, Shiva/linga worship destroyed; women leave husbands' service; men use vashikarana on others' wives; Alakshmi enters, Lakshmi leaves; they lose strength (5.49-63) | same in substance |
| Japa | Vishnu 1.5 crore Rudra japa in water (ch.6); gods 1 crore "ॐ नमः शिवाय शुभं शुभं कुरु कुरु शिवाय नमः ॐ" (ch.7); Kumbhodara beats gods at the door | not told |
| Chariot | Vishwakarma; full list ch.8 (Sun 12 spokes, Moon 16, seasons rims, Mandara seat, Udaya/Asta poles, year speed, ayanas axle-joints, Brahma charioteer, Pranava whip, Sumeru bow, Shesha string, Sarasvati bow-bell, Vishnu arrow, Agni tip, 4 Vedas horses, rivers chamara-bearers, rishis) | earth = chariot; Shiva laughs at it |
| Horses fall | Vedas fall on their heads, earth shakes (9.1-7) | fall to their knees; Brahman beyond Vedas |
| THE BULL | SHESHA as bull lifts it a moment, then falls to his knees (9.8-9) | VISHNU becomes the bull (Shivananda Lahari "बाणत्वं वृषभत्वं...") |
| Pashupati | gods must accept being pashus first; Pashupata vow (9.13-24) | not told |
| Delay | Shiva stands motionless 100,000 yrs; Ganesha on his thumb blocks the aim; sky-voice; Bhadrakali; Ganesha worshipped; cities merge (10.1-14) | not told |
| Burning | gods: a glance would do, use the arrow for our fame; Pashupatastra; Abhijit; names himself, twangs, fires; Vishnu-arrow with fire-tip burns the three; ash falls on earth (10.20-28) | third eye about to open; Linga Purana verse (Soma, Vishnu, Kalagni, Vayu in the arrow) asks to be released |
| Tarakaksha's last words | "हे भव! ... हमारी बुद्धि जन्म-जन्मान्तरमें आपकी भक्तिसे भावित रहे" (10.32-33) | not told |
| Who burns | all at Shiva's command: children, old, women, vehicles; women embracing husbands, asleep (10.34-38) | "with the rakshasas" |
| Survivors | only Maya, refuge-taking devotee (10.39-40); those still worshipping Shiva become gana-lords (10.44) | |
| After | gods praise; Maya asks bhakti, sent to Vitala (ch.12); Mundis fear hell, told to hide in the desert till Kali (12.21-32) | flower-rain, dundubhis (his padyam) |
| Kartika Purnima | NOT mentioned anywhere in ch.1-12 | (tradition: Tripurari Purnima) |
| Shiva smiles the cities to ash | NOT in this edition | no |
