# Gita Jayanti Shorts: voice-over scripts (all 18)

Structure, following the 4.7 edit: hook question → "Lord Krishna answers this in the Bhagavad Gita." → **[Vāgdhenu chant]** → "Which means…" + the quoted translation → "In simple words, Krishna is saying…" → reflection.
Narrator: Shardul K (ElevenLabs, eleven_v4). Music: Saffron Morning, continuous from the first frame. The exact text lives in `vo/scripts.json` (that is what is recorded and captioned).

Notes kept for accuracy: 1.1 is Dhritarashtra speaking (no "Krishna answers"); 12.13 lists the qualities and "dear to Me" closes 12.14; 18.66 "setting aside all dharmas" (Gita Press glosses it as offering all duties to Him, not abandoning action); 4.7 text is the user's own.


## Day 1: 1.1
**[Shardul]**
The Bhagavad Gita doesn't begin with Krishna… It begins with a blind king, asking a question he is afraid to hear answered.

**[Vāgdhenu]**
धर्मक्षेत्रे कुरुक्षेत्रे समवेता युयुत्सवः।
मामकाः पाण्डवाश्चैव किमकुर्वत सञ्जय॥

**[Shardul]**
Which means…

"On the holy field of Kurukshetra, gathered and eager to fight, what did my sons and the sons of Pandu do, O Sanjaya?"

In simple words, a father asks about the war… but listen to how he asks it. My sons. And Pandu's sons.

Before a single arrow flies, the divide is already in his heart. And that is where every war truly begins.


## Day 2: 2.47
**[Shardul]**
Why do we work so hard… and still feel empty, anxious, never satisfied?

Lord Krishna answers this in the Bhagavad Gita.

**[Vāgdhenu]**
कर्मण्येवाधिकारस्ते मा फलेषु कदाचन।
मा कर्मफलहेतुर्भूर्मा ते सङ्गोऽस्त्वकर्मणि॥

**[Shardul]**
Which means…

"Your right is to action alone, never to its fruits. Let not the fruit be your motive, nor cling to inaction."

In simple words, Krishna is saying: give your work everything you have, but don't chain your peace to the result.

The result was never in your hands. The effort always was.

This isn't a lesson in giving up… it's the secret of working without fear.


## Day 3: 3.35
**[Shardul]**
Why does living someone else's life feel so empty… and so frightening?

Lord Krishna answers this in the Bhagavad Gita.

**[Vāgdhenu]**
श्रेयान्स्वधर्मो विगुणः परधर्मात्स्वनुष्ठितात्।
स्वधर्मे निधनं श्रेयः परधर्मो भयावहः॥

**[Shardul]**
Which means…

"Better is your own dharma, though imperfect, than another's dharma done well. Better is death in your own dharma; another's dharma is full of fear."

In simple words, Krishna is saying: walk your own path, even if you stumble, rather than live a borrowed life perfectly.

Comparison feels like ambition. Krishna calls it fear.


## Day 4: 4.7
**[Shardul]**
What happens when evil starts winning… when truth is forgotten, and injustice takes over?

Lord Krishna answers this in the Bhagavad Gita.

**[Vāgdhenu]**
यदा यदा हि धर्मस्य ग्लानिर्भवति भारत।
अभ्युत्थानमधर्मस्य तदात्मानं सृजाम्यहम्॥

**[Shardul]**
Which means…

"Whenever righteousness declines, and unrighteousness rises, I manifest Myself."

In simple words, God is saying: whenever the world loses its way, whenever darkness grows stronger than goodness, I come to restore balance.

Evil may rise. Truth may suffer. But neither is forgotten by the divine.

This isn't just a promise of hope… it's a reminder that darkness never gets the final word.


## Day 5: 5.18
**[Shardul]**
How do you recognise a truly wise person… not by what they know, but by how they see?

Lord Krishna answers this in the Bhagavad Gita.

**[Vāgdhenu]**
विद्याविनयसम्पन्ने ब्राह्मणे गवि हस्तिनि।
शुनि चैव श्वपाके च पण्डिताः समदर्शिनः॥

**[Shardul]**
Which means…

"The wise see with an equal eye a learned and humble Brahmin, a cow, an elephant, a dog, and an outcaste."

In simple words, Krishna is saying: the wise see the same divine Self shining in every living being.

Birth, status, form… these are only the surface.

True wisdom isn't knowing more… it's seeing the one light in all.


## Day 6: 6.5
**[Shardul]**
Who is your greatest enemy… the one who can pull you down further than anyone else?

Lord Krishna answers this in the Bhagavad Gita.

**[Vāgdhenu]**
उद्धरेदात्मनात्मानं नात्मानमवसादयेत्।
आत्मैव ह्यात्मनो बन्धुरात्मैव रिपुरात्मनः॥

**[Shardul]**
Which means…

"Lift yourself by your own self; never let yourself sink. For the self alone is your friend, and the self alone is your enemy."

In simple words, Krishna is saying: no one can raise you, or ruin you, the way your own mind can.

Be the hand that lifts you, not the weight that drags you down.

Your greatest battle isn't out there… it's within.


## Day 7: 7.19
**[Shardul]**
After countless lifetimes… what does the wise soul finally understand?

Lord Krishna answers this in the Bhagavad Gita.

**[Vāgdhenu]**
बहूनां जन्मनामन्ते ज्ञानवान्मां प्रपद्यते।
वासुदेवः सर्वमिति स महात्मा सुदुर्लभः॥

**[Shardul]**
Which means…

"At the end of many births, the wise one surrenders to Me, knowing: Vasudeva is everything. Such a great soul is very rare."

In simple words, Krishna is saying: the highest wisdom is to see the Divine in all that exists… and to surrender.

It takes many lives to learn. Very few ever do.

This isn't knowledge from books… it's the journey of a soul.


## Day 8: 8.7
**[Shardul]**
In your final moment… what will your mind hold on to?

Lord Krishna answers this in the Bhagavad Gita.

**[Vāgdhenu]**
तस्मात्सर्वेषु कालेषु मामनुस्मर युध्य च।
मय्यर्पितमनोबुद्धिर्मामेवैष्यस्यसंशयम्॥

**[Shardul]**
Which means…

"Therefore, at all times, remember Me, and fight. With your mind and intellect offered to Me, you will surely come to Me."

In simple words, Krishna is saying: keep the Divine in your heart, and still do your duty.

He doesn't ask Arjuna to leave the battlefield. He asks him to remember while he fights.

Devotion isn't running from life… it's living it with God in mind.


## Day 9: 9.26
**[Shardul]**
What does God really want from you… gold? Grand rituals? Lavish offerings?

Lord Krishna answers this in the Bhagavad Gita.

**[Vāgdhenu]**
पत्रं पुष्पं फलं तोयं यो मे भक्त्या प्रयच्छति।
तदहं भक्त्युपहृतमश्नामि प्रयतात्मनः॥

**[Shardul]**
Which means…

"Whoever offers Me with love a leaf, a flower, a fruit, or a little water, that loving gift of a pure heart, I accept."

In simple words, Krishna is saying: even the smallest offering, given with love, reaches Me.

The Divine doesn't measure the gift. He sees the heart that gives it.

A single leaf, offered with love… is enough.


## Day 10: 10.20
**[Shardul]**
Where does God live… in a temple? Beyond the sky?

Lord Krishna answers this in the Bhagavad Gita.

**[Vāgdhenu]**
अहमात्मा गुडाकेश सर्वभूताशयस्थितः।
अहमादिश्च मध्यं च भूतानामन्त एव च॥

**[Shardul]**

"I am the Self, O Arjuna, seated in the hearts of all beings. I am their beginning, their middle, and their end."

In simple words, Krishna is saying: the Divine isn't far away. He lives within every heart, from the first breath to the last.

You don't have to search far… only deep.


## Day 11: 11.32
**[Shardul]**
Arjuna sees the universal form, blazing, devouring armies… and trembling, he asks: who are You?

Lord Krishna answers this in the Bhagavad Gita.

**[Vāgdhenu]**
कालोऽस्मि लोकक्षयकृत्प्रवृद्धो लोकान्समाहर्तुमिह प्रवृत्तः।
ऋतेऽपि त्वां न भविष्यन्ति सर्वे येऽवस्थिताः प्रत्यनीकेषु योधाः॥

**[Shardul]**
Which means…

"I am Time, the mighty destroyer of worlds, come here to consume all people. Even without you, none of the warriors arrayed against you will survive."

In simple words, Krishna is saying: everything that is born, Time will take.

The outcome was decided before the first arrow flew.

Arjuna isn't the destroyer… he is only the instrument.


## Day 12: 12.13
**[Shardul]**
What kind of person is truly dear to God?

Lord Krishna answers this in the Bhagavad Gita.

**[Vāgdhenu]**
अद्वेष्टा सर्वभूतानां मैत्रः करुण एव च।
निर्ममो निरहङ्कारः समदुःखसुखः क्षमी॥

**[Shardul]**
Which means…

"One who hates no being, who is friendly and compassionate, free from 'mine' and from ego, equal in pleasure and pain, and forgiving."

In simple words, Krishna is saying: no hatred, kindness for all, no ego, and a forgiving heart.

And in the very next verse, He says: such a devotee is dear to Me.

Not wealth. Not status… a gentle heart.


## Day 13: 13.2
**[Shardul]**
Krishna says your body is a field… so who is the one who knows it?

Lord Krishna answers this in the Bhagavad Gita.

**[Vāgdhenu]**
क्षेत्रज्ञं चापि मां विद्धि सर्वक्षेत्रेषु भारत।
क्षेत्रक्षेत्रज्ञयोर्ज्ञानं यत्तज्ज्ञानं मतं मम॥

**[Shardul]**
Which means…

"Know Me as the knower of the field in all fields, O Bharata. The knowledge of the field and its knower, that, in My view, is true knowledge."

In simple words, Krishna is saying: the body is the field, and the knower within every being is the Divine.

The body changes… the knower within never does.


## Day 14: 14.5
**[Shardul]**
What are the three invisible ropes that bind every soul?

Lord Krishna answers this in the Bhagavad Gita.

**[Vāgdhenu]**
सत्त्वं रजस्तम इति गुणाः प्रकृतिसम्भवाः।
निबध्नन्ति महाबाहो देहे देहिनमव्ययम्॥

**[Shardul]**

"Sattva, rajas and tamas, the three qualities born of nature, O mighty-armed one, bind the imperishable soul to the body."

In simple words, Krishna is saying: goodness, passion and inertia… all three tie the eternal soul to the body.

Even goodness is a rope. A golden one… but still a rope.

ममैवांशो जीवलोके जीवभूतः सनातनः।

## Day 16: 16.21
**[Shardul]**
Krishna names three gates that lead straight to hell… do you know what they are?

Lord Krishna answers this in the Bhagavad Gita.

**[Vāgdhenu]**
त्रिविधं नरकस्येदं द्वारं नाशनमात्मनः।
कामः क्रोधस्तथा लोभस्तस्मादेतत्त्रयं त्यजेत्॥

**[Shardul]**
Which means…

"Desire, anger and greed: these three are the gates of hell, destroyers of the self. Therefore, give up all three."

In simple words, Krishna is saying: lust, anger and greed don't just hurt others… they destroy you from within.

Every fall begins at one of these three gates.

Close them… and you guard your soul.


## Day 17: 17.20
**[Shardul]**
What is true charity… is every gift really a gift?

Lord Krishna answers this in the Bhagavad Gita.

**[Vāgdhenu]**
दातव्यमिति यद्दानं दीयतेऽनुपकारिणे।
देशे काले च पात्रे च तद्दानं सात्त्विकं स्मृतम्॥

**[Shardul]**
Which means…

"A gift given because giving is right, to one who cannot repay, at the proper place and time, to a worthy person… that gift is called sattvic."

In simple words, Krishna is saying: give because it is right. Not for praise. Not for return.

The purest gift is the one that asks for nothing back.


## Day 18: 18.66
**[Shardul]**
After all His teachings… what is Krishna's final, most secret word to Arjuna, and to all of us?

Here it is, from the Bhagavad Gita.

**[Vāgdhenu]**
सर्वधर्मान्परित्यज्य मामेकं शरणं व्रज।
अहं त्वा सर्वपापेभ्यो मोक्षयिष्यामि मा शुचः॥

**[Shardul]**
Which means…

"Setting aside all dharmas, take refuge in Me alone. I will free you from all sins. Do not grieve."

In simple words, Krishna is saying: let go of every support you cling to, and surrender to Me. I will carry your burden.

After all the battles, all the questions… the Gita ends with a promise: do not grieve.
