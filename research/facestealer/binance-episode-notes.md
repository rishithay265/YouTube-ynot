# Saved for a separate episode: the Binance theft ("TraderTraitor")

Cut from the FaceStealer v2 edit so that video stays on one story. The DOJ calls this a **separate** case.

## What the v1 cut said (verbatim narration)

> The same day, the Justice Department described a separate case. On or about October twenty-ninth, it says North
> Korean military hackers, called TraderTraitor by the FBI, stole more than six billion dollars from the exchange
> Binance. It's a different attack, and the Justice Department calls it separate from FaceStealer. Authorities have
> frozen more than two point seven billion dollars, and got court approval to seize or restrain over one point two billion.

## Sourced facts (DOJ press release 26-1356, 12 Nov 2026; page capture at `research/facestealer/doj.png`)

- "Separately from the FaceStealer campaign, on or about Oct. 29, members of the North Korean military, referred to by
  the FBI as TraderTraitor, stole more than $6 billion worth of virtual assets from the Binance cryptocurrency exchange."
- Freeze of more than $2.7 billion; court-authorized seizure and restraint of more than $1.2 billion.
- Quote (Principal Deputy AAG M. Scott Proctor): "North Korea's government bankrolls its rogue regime and illicit
  weapons program through cryptocurrency theft."
- Quote (AAG A. Tysen Duva): the Criminal Division "immediately halt[ed] about half of this historic breach."
- Some press reports say $6.3 billion; the DOJ text says "more than $6 billion". The DOJ does not say AI was used.
