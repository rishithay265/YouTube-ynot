## Files to upload
1. `kubera_ijhs.pdf`: the manuscript, for reviewers
2. `kubera_ijhs_source.zip`: `kubera_ijhs.tex`, `refs.bib`, `sn-jnl.cls`, `sn-basic.bst`, `figs/`
3. Cover letter: paste `COVER_LETTER.md` into the cover-letter box, or upload it as a PDF

## Steps
1. Open the journal's page on link.springer.com (search "Indian Journal of History of Science") and click **Submit manuscript**. Create an account with the same name and ORCID.
2. Article type: **Original/Research Article**.
3. Enter the title, abstract and keywords exactly as in the PDF.
4. Author: you only, corresponding author, with your ORCID and e-mail.
5. Funding: **none**. Competing interests: **none**.
6. Upload the files above. If the system rebuilds the PDF from LaTeX, check that its output matches `kubera_ijhs.pdf`.
7. Suggested reviewers: optional. If you add any, choose scholars of Indian mathematics or Sanskrit with no connection to you; never suggest friends.
8. Approve the system-built PDF and submit.

## After submission
- First decision: Springer lists a median of about a week for the first decision (often a desk check). Full review usually takes months.
- Expect "major revisions": answer every reviewer point in a response letter. I can help draft it.
- Do **not** submit to any other journal while this one is under review.
- If it is accepted, add the journal DOI to the Zenodo record as a new version, under Related works, "Is published in".
