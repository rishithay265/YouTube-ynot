# Sources: Kubera Yantra study

Confidence scale:
- **High**: we read the passage ourselves in a primary edition or e-text, or computed the result.
- **Medium**: we read it through OCR or a translation only, or the source is reputable but we read only part of it.
- **Low**: secondary or popular source, or a bibliographic record only.

The access date for all URLs is 2026-11-25. Copies of the passages we relied on are in `excerpts/`.
